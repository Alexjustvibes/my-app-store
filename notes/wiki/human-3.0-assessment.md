# Human 3.0 Development Assessment — Results

Type: personal self-assessment (Koe-lineage **Mind / Body / Spirit / Vocation** framework). Results for the human, 2026-06-09. A companion diagnostic to [[my-reader-profile-entp]] (MBTI).

## Headline
- **Metatype: The Aligned Monk** — Mind, Body, and Vocation are quietly well-run (pragmatic thinking that updates on evidence, physical fundamentals that hold under stress, genuinely chosen work). The catch: a three-legged structure on a missing fourth leg. **Spirit isn't damaged — it's *unstaffed.***
- **Lifestyle archetype: The Workaholic (early-stage)** — work is the primary, nearly only, source of meaning. Mind and Body aren't neglected (so it's less obviously self-destructive), but **identity and meaning are single-sourced.**

## Quadrant breakdown
- **Mind — The Pragmatic Synthesizer** · Discovery, Mid→High. *Strength:* actually updates beliefs on contradicting evidence and acts on it (the "5"/TikTok example). *Gap:* the filter is pure utility ("does this help my situation") with no anchor outside it, and hasn't been tested by anything that threatened his identity — the belief-changes named were low-stakes.
- **Body — The Quiet Maintainer** · Discovery (paused), Mid. *Strength:* sleep/food/movement hold under stress — a real foundation. *Gap:* no deliberate training; knowledge dormant by choice, but dormant doesn't stay sharp forever.
- **Spirit — The Unstaffed Room** · Dissonance (not yet felt as dissonance — more an absence he hasn't had to look at), **Low (never built, not regressed).** *Strength:* relationships exist and are fine when engaged. *Gap:* meaning almost entirely work-derived; relationships backgrounded; **no community dimension at all.** *To his credit:* no false transformation — he left the room empty rather than faking depth. **This quadrant is quietly setting the ceiling on everything else.**
- **Vocation — The Genuine Article** · Discovery, Mid-High. *Strength:* rare alignment — he'd do the same work without money or status (cf. [[concepts/alignment]], [[concepts/job-career-calling]] calling-level). *Gap:* two scoreboards (fulfillment + money) with no stress-test for when they diverge. **Currently propping up his sense of self almost single-handedly.**

## Cross-quadrant dynamics
- **Primary block:** Spirit isn't blocking anything *yet* — but it's the single point of failure. Vocation is strong enough to cover for it *for now.*
- **Cascade warning:** if Vocation hits a real wall (a slow stretch, a plateau, a flopped launch), there's no Spirit to absorb it — it goes **straight into identity, because work *is* identity right now.**
- **Hidden pattern:** "situational, no absolute truth" in both Mind and Spirit → nothing outside his own utility calculus is load-bearing. Invisible when work goes well; no external anchor when it doesn't.
- **Unlock:** not "build Spirit from zero" — the relationships *exist*, just backgrounded. It's a **redirect, not a rebuild.**

## Core problem (per the assessment)
**Spirit is running on empty while Vocation carries all the weight — and that's the root, not a symptom.** The fix isn't "work less"; it's directing some of the attention he already spends well toward relationships that already exist but sit idle.

## Strategy (30 / 90 / 180–360)
- **30 days (recognition):** one real non-work conversation a day; one weekly activity with family/friends with zero productive output; success = noticing, unprompted, that a relationship gave him something work didn't.
- **90 days (implementation):** apply the pragmatic-evaluation skill to relationships (what actually helps *them*); milestone = can name a *person* he'd call if Vocation had a bad month.
- **6–12 months (integration):** Workaholic → Integrated; Spirit from unstaffed to genuinely present; Vocation stays the engine but stops solely holding up identity.

## Warnings
- **Regression trigger:** a real business downturn hits as an *identity crisis*, not a business problem (no Spirit buffer).
- **Lifestyle trap:** "I'd do this work for free" is NOT proof the structure is fine. Alignment with work doesn't substitute for a life outside it — it just delays when he notices the gap.
- **AI-atrophy risk (Mind):** if AI increasingly synthesizes *for* him rather than *with* him, his synthesis skill atrophies quietly. Use it to sharpen his own thinking, not replace the step where he does it. (Directly relevant to *this wiki* — see below.)

## Immediate next action (from the assessment)
**Today, before anything work-related:** message one person (family/friend) and make a real plan to do something *this week* with zero connection to content, business, or the shop. Not a call about his day — an actual plan.
