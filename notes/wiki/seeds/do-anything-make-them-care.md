## Hook (the counterintuitive claim, in one line)
You can build a whole life around *literally anything* you'd do forever — the topic was never the problem. Your only real job is to make people care about it.

## Who this is for (the specific, often-overlooked person)
The person who thinks they have nothing to offer the internet, or who chases trending topics they don't care about to "grow an audience" — because they assume nobody would care about their real interests (his own "5"/TikTok moment: assumed nobody cared about philosophy/psychology, was proven wrong).

## The argument
- The constraint isn't the topic — you can do *anything*. Proof: someone out there makes people care about the exact thing you dismissed as too niche.
- The real skill is **persuasion / making people care** — and that's learnable, not innate.
- **The turn (the warning that makes it non-generic):** since you can do anything *and* do it forever, it has to be something you'd actually do forever — so authenticity isn't optional, it's structural. Chasing an audience with stuff you don't care about is self-defeating; your job is to make people care about what *you* want to make.

What do you really want out of life?

I think ever single human being has come to this question in their minds at some point right after being met with the dark reality of death at least once.

Life becomes something really really precious for a good lucid few seconds after you really start grasping the reality of death but we soon forget the answers we make when we are met with the constraints of reality. 

And instead of meeting these constraints and saying well these constraints exist but I can still get what I want out of life we give up immediately and forget or at least those who were born with low agency tend to do so. 

Constraints and entropy go hand in hand. Entropy burdens us because it reveals our constraints.

I never really stopped asking that question, what do I want out of life? It's been in the back of my mind and I never really let go of that question despite my peers and my parents and a majority of society saying we don't ever get to choose what to do with our lives, we simply get pushed by the invisible hand of life with no choice in the matter.

But I couldn't let go of the question.

I was scrolling on tiktok one day and I came across the profile of a tiktoker named 5 he was creating videos surrounding topics i cared about yet deemed far too uninteresting for a platform like Tiktok, he was talking about philosophy, psychology and arguing with people about these not very interesting topics and still succeeding on the platform, so I asked myself why couldn't I do that? He's a human just like me.

So it dawned on me, I wanted to do exactly what he was doing. Making content that educated people about things while sort of educating them at the same time, with videos. I just didn't really think anyone would really care about my ideas so I just did other things and wasted my time on this earth when I really wanted to do that.

That's really what I wanted to talk about today. You basically have to assume that the things you think are interesting and valuable are interesting and valuable to other people if you ever want to make a living doing what you actually want to do in life. When you assume nobody will care it just gives you an excuse to never start or worse you start making content about things you never really cared about in a chase of clout or the approval of other people. 

So the unconventional wisdom here is that you can literally make money doing anything, as long as you convince people to care with persuasion. You just need to have the self-awareness to really understand what you want to do with your life.

Take personality tests, like myers-briggs, examine what you already do and determine which thing that you do is the most useful for others yet most enjoyable for you, think about what you enjoyed to do as a child and see if you can find traces of it in skills that you are drawn to. 

It's going to take a lot of trial and error and experimentation but you NEED to take this time because if you don't you will waste years of your life doing the lifes work of someone else who isn't you.

After you figure out what you want to do, if it isn't something that requires a traditional path get really good at it and start talking about it online and creating content about it  and persuade others to care about your work, research how you can start making an income doing it.
