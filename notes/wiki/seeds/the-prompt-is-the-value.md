## Hook (the counterintuitive claim, in one line)
**Proven hook (use this one): "Ideas are the new oil."** — the 65-like framing. Concrete, ownable, resource-metaphor.
Alt (the note version): AI didn't make your work worthless — it made the *output* worthless. The value moved upstream, to the one thing it can't generate: the prompt. The taste, the judgment, the question.

## Why "ideas are the new oil" beat "the prompt is the value" (9× the likes — the lesson)
Same idea, three upgrades worth stealing every time:
- **Concrete metaphor > abstract noun.** "Oil" is a resource you can picture and covet; "prompt/output" is jargon. ([[frameworks/attention-capture-tactics]].)
- **It named the villain-relief the reader feels:** "raw skill isn't what holds you back — it's the idea underneath." That's the recognition line — it frees the reader who thinks they lack skill.
- **It added the *what-do-I-do*:** writing is the idea-generation engine (below). The prompt-note stated a truth; the oil-video handed over a practice.

## Who this is for (the specific, often-overlooked person)
The person anxious that AI makes their skill/output obsolete — and the person still optimizing *output quality* (polishing the essay, perfecting the deliverable) when outputs are now infinite and free.

## The argument
- When anyone can generate infinite competent outputs, outputs stop being scarce → they're commodities → their price falls to ~zero.
- What stays scarce is the *input*: knowing what's worth making, asking the right question, having the taste to recognize the good output from the slop. The **prompt** (in the broad sense — judgment + intent).
- This is [[concepts/mind-as-scaffolding]] / [[seeds/the-constraint-was-never-the-code]] stated for the AI era: "the constraint was never execution, it's clarity of thinking." Now literally true — the prompt *is* the clarity.
- The turn: stop optimizing the output; optimize the *taste and judgment behind the prompt.* That's the only appreciating asset. (Ties to [[concepts/resonance]] — the fingerprint AI can't fake; [[seeds/worth-tied-to-irreplaceability-is-the-trap]] — your worth is your judgment, not your output.)
- **Writing IS the idea-generation engine** (the 65-like video's addition — and the bridge straight to his craft). "You have to create a new, original idea for your brain — the AI can't do that for you." The tool for that is *writing*: "create new idea experiments within your mind, within the writing itself." So the value chain is: **writing → ideas → the idea-file → AI executes.** This makes writing the highest-leverage skill in the AI age, not an obsolete one — the exact opposite of what anxious creators believe. (Ties to [[concepts/two-modes-of-attention]] creativity=connection, [[my-domain-of-mastery]] craft=writing, [[concepts/polymathy]].)
- The clean payoff line: **you don't out-execute AI, you out-*originate* it.** Ideas are the scarce input; execution is the free commodity.
