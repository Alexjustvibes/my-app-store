## Synthesis (TWO roots)
- **Root A — the vision/commitment/shipping cluster** (#4, #8, #9, #10): the ENTP pattern of breadth + ideas outpacing finishing. Blocks *vocation* progress. Cure: **pick one thing and ship it** ([[concepts/the-arena]], [[concepts/developmental-stages]]). #1, #5, #7 fall once you ship.
- **Root B — Spirit / relationships (#6)**: identified by [[human-3.0-assessment]] as the deeper root — the *single point of failure*. Doesn't block anything now (Vocation covers for it), but sets the ceiling and turns any business setback into an identity crisis. Cure: **redirect attention to relationships that already exist** (a redirect, not a rebuild).
- **The tension between them:** Root A's cure is "pour everything into shipping/vocation." Root B's cure is "put some attention *outside* work." Both are true. The integration: **build the vocation engine AND staff the empty room — the goals shouldn't be 100% vocation** (they currently are; see [[goals]] / [[vision]]).

The reframe that matters: you're not behind. **Every problem here is shared by your reader** (the aspiring creator / betrayed high-achiever / fellow ENTP). Documenting your way through them *is* the content and the product. You're not stuck — you're accumulating inventory.
