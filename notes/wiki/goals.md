# Goals (running)

Goals = the [[vision]] broken into time-scoped chunks (Koe's 10 pillars). Big = direction, small = clarity. **Stubborn on vision, loose on details** — goals will change; that's fine. Set 2026-06-09. **Output-only goals** (because the #1 problem is shipping — [[problems-log]] #8).

| Scale | Goal |
|---|---|
| **Direction** | FMS launched to a validated audience; a community of value creators ([[vision]]) |
| **This year** | An audience **just large enough to make *any* money** (first dollar = the survival-level win) + **validate FMS demand** (the hard part — his stated focus) |
| **Weekly** | **1 published newsletter** |
| **DAILY LEVER** | **3 published notes/day** (target) · **floor: 1/day, non-negotiable** — protect the streak; a missed day is allowed to be a 1-note day, never a 0 |

## Why this set is right
- **Output-only** — every goal is "ship X," none is "consume X." Direct cure for [[problems-log]] #8 / #10.
- **The daily lever attacks 5 problems at once:** audience (#1), content-creation skill (#7), shipping (#8), over-consuming (#10), and demand-testing (#2).
- **The notes ARE the demand validation.** Each note/newsletter probes what resonates; the signals to watch (per [[fms]] launch trigger + [[notes-performance]]) are *recognition* and *"I'd buy this"* — not raw likes. So his hardest goal (validate demand) is accomplished *by* hitting the daily lever, not as a separate task.
- **Volume + back-catalogue** (Koe / [[concepts/media-as-leverage]]): "opportunities from 30K tweets, not the 3 you post today." 3/day × a year ≈ 1,000 notes. The library is the asset.

## The floor matters (ENTP safeguard)
3/day is the target, but the *streak* matters more than the count. The failure mode ([[my-reader-profile-entp]]) is all-or-nothing collapse — miss a day, quit the habit. So the **floor is 1/day, non-negotiable.** A bad day is a 1-note day. The chain never breaks to zero.

## Deliberately out (2026-06-09)
**Spirit / relationships** — [[human-3.0-assessment]] flags it as the root problem, but the human consciously deprioritized it for now. Goals stay all-vocation *by choice, eyes open* (not oversight). Revisit if/when the business hits a real wall.
