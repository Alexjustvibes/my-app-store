# Vision & Anti-Vision

The two poles of the 10 pillars ([[sources/koe-purpose-and-profit]] ch. 8). Articulated by the human 2026-06-09. Every decision filters through these.

## Anti-Vision (the future I refuse)
- A **job I hate** — and I'll hate *every* job working under someone else, because I'll have no leverage.
- **No community.**
- **Not working for something greater than myself** — something that doesn't help humanity evolve forward.

(The detailed, lived version of this is the [[problems-log]] — the specific problems are the anti-vision made concrete.)

## Vision (the future I'm building)
- An **online business** of my own.
- To be so **self-developed that I can sell solutions to problems I've already encountered and defeated** in my own life. *(= Koe's "you are the niche" / self-monetization; [[frameworks/the-experience-model]].)*
- A **community of other value creators** doing the same thing.
- Work that helps humanity reach a point where **everyone has their own business / their own thing** — their own agency.
- "It all starts with me — so I solve my own problems first."

## Why this coheres with everything
This vision *is* the mission, made personal and concrete:
- "Everyone has their own thing / their own agency" = **leading others to [[concepts/alignment|aligned freedom]]** (CLAUDE.md mission).
- "Sell solutions to problems I've defeated" = the [[problems-log]] → product loop = Koe's value creation.
- "It starts with me, solve my problems first" = Koe's **"free yourself, then free others."**
- "Community of value creators" = the [[concepts/decentralized-education|decentralized]] Second Renaissance; the [[fms]] audience grown up.
- The vehicle = [[fms]] (teach people to create their own thing from scratch).

So: your genuine interest (creativity/making), your product (FMS), your mission (aligned freedom), and now your vision (a world of value creators) are all **one thing** pointed at one word — freedom — with *value creation* as the verb.

## Mission — the bridge (anti-vision → vision)
Per Koe, the **mission is the path between what you don't want and what you do** — the life's work, the daily doing. Derived from the two poles above:

> **Draft mission (human to confirm/word):** *Free myself by turning my own solved problems into value — then lead others to do the same — until having your own thing (aligned freedom) is the norm, not the exception.*

The bridge in one line: **value creation, self → others.** Solve my problem → ship the solution → free myself → free others. Repeat at each level.

**Relationship to the CLAUDE.md north star:** "leading others to aligned freedom" is the *others-facing half* of this mission (the endpoint action). The draft above adds the *starts-with-me half* (free myself first, via solving + shipping). Same bridge, fuller statement. (Open: keep "leading others to aligned freedom" as the held-above-all mission in CLAUDE.md, or update it to this fuller version? — human's call.)

## The trap inside it (important)
*"I need to solve my personal problems first before I get there"* is half-right and half-dangerous. Right: free yourself before you can free others (Koe: "at the start, selfishness is selfless"). **Dangerous:** if "first" means *solve ALL of them before I start*, it becomes a permission-to-wait — and that feeds your #1 problem (can't ship, [[problems-log]] #8). 

Koe's actual model: you don't wait until you've arrived. You teach **from one stage ahead** of the person behind you, *while still climbing* ([[concepts/developmental-stages]]). You solve your *current* problem, ship that solution now, then climb to the next. You're never "done," and you never wait. So the vision starts not with "solve everything," but with **"solve and ship the first one."**
