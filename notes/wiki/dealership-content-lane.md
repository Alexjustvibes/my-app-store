# Work & the Dealership Content Lane

Real-world context (2026-06-09). The human's day job + a content-strategy decision that came out of exploring an AI-consulting business.

## The situation
- **Day job:** Tim's Saw Shop — a **Bobcat equipment dealership** (servicing + sales).
- **What he's already built there (the proof / the skill):** AI/automation workflows — **HelloClient intake, missed-call auto-text, lead capture.** These are a credible **before/after case study** even without revenue proof yet.

## The decision (explored → landed)
Explored selling **AI-workflow / vibe-coding services** to local Okanogan County businesses. Reframed twice:
1. "I need proof of usefulness before I can market myself" (reps before leverage).
2. "I want a product I fully control and full revenue on" — examined and **set aside** (a brand-new solo service business doesn't actually give more control or revenue than the established one on day one).

**Landed on: don't start a separate consulting business. Instead, *document the Tim's/Bobcat work itself* — including problems still being solved — as a new content lane, *additive* to the existing writing/video pipeline (not a replacement).**

## The real issue (why content energy is low after work)
Not the hours — it's **open, unsolved loops running in the background all day** and draining attention:
- **Ownership/compensation terms** with Tim's are undefined (limbo).
- A current **~2-week gap with zero Bobcat servicing leads.**

> Connection: Koe's creativity-vs-productivity source names *"open loops of tasks"* as a direct drain on conscious bandwidth ([[sources/dan-koe-creativity-vs-productivity]]). So the lever for post-work content energy isn't fewer hours — it's **closing or externalizing the open loops.** And **documenting them as content externalizes them** — turning the drain into material. Two birds.

## Live problem being documented (content material)
The **servicing-lead drought.** An automated call to 50 Bobcat equipment buyers about servicing **didn't convert** — likely because automated calls read as spam, there was no seasonal/urgency hook, and the sample was small. Next steps: personal text/call, lead with **Ruben joining as mechanic**, tie to the **Sept 5 open house.** (This in-progress, unsolved story = exactly the kind of authentic "document your losses" content Koe's authenticity pillar calls for.)

## Why this lane is strong
- **Real work + real proof + real problems = authentic content** ([[frameworks/social-leverage]] authenticity pillar; [[frameworks/the-experience-model]] — document your projects).
- **Concrete AI-build proof for [[fms]] credibility** — he's not just theorizing about "you can build with AI," he's doing it at a real business. This is [[seeds/the-constraint-was-never-the-code]] *made real by him.*
- **Partially solves [[problems-log]] #7** (no sellable skill): he now has a demonstrable AI-automation skill with a case study.
- It's a **second content lane** alongside his philosophy/psychology lane ([[my-domain-of-mastery]]) — concrete + practical, balancing the abstract.
