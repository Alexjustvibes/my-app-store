## 1. The Content Tree (Domain of Mastery template)
**Source:** Dan Koe — [[frameworks/domain-of-mastery]]. · **Status:** ✅ USED — filled 2026-08-30.

**Structure:** `YOU` (top) → **3 pillars** (interests) → each pillar has **3 sub-topics** → each drops to **~6 specific content ideas** → all funnel into `CONTENT` (bottom). Caption: fill top-down with your interests → unlimited content ideas.

**His filled version:** pillars = his three Koe slots — **CREATE** (genuine / creativity), **BECOME** (developmental / psychology), **BUILD** (money / teaching creating online), all orbiting *freedom*. Idea rows = his real seeds. Built as the **"Freedom Engine"** artifact (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0226d601-d887-47db-b4e9-d010f28d180f) and grounded in [[my-domain-of-mastery]]. Use it to generate *topics*.

## 2. The One-Person Business Map (self → business)
**Source:** Dan Koe. · **Status:** 🆕 shared + ingested 2026-08-30 → full page: [[frameworks/one-person-business-map]].

**Structure:** two columns, left (your life) → right (the business), 4 rows:
- **GOALS** (what do you want out of life?) → **BRAND** (what are you leading people towards?)
- **PROBLEMS** (what's standing in your way?) → **CONTENT** (what roadblocks will they hit?)
- **SYSTEMS** (how did you solve it?) → **PRODUCT** (how can they solve it faster?)
- **BENEFITS** (how has it impacted your life?) → **MARKETING** (why should they care?)
- Quote: *"Sensible people get paid for doing what they enjoy doing." — Alan Watts*

**Why he saved it:** it's his whole wiki as a build-spec — his [[problems-log]] literally *is* the CONTENT column. See [[frameworks/one-person-business-map]] for the full mapping onto his pages. Use it to generate the *offer + reason to buy*.

## How the two templates pair
The **Content Tree** generates *topics* (what to write about). The **One-Person Business Map** generates the *offer* (what to sell + why they should care). Topic engine + business engine. Both are Koe's; his originality is in what he pours into them.
