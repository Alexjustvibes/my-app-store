# Concept: The One-Human Business

STUB — Koe flags a full breakdown in a future letter; seeded here from [[sources/koe-digital-renaissance-polymaths]] so later letters have a home. Expand when they land.

## Definition (working)
One person running a whole company, AI-leveraged: **content** on social media to attract an audience around your life's work + **code** to build the product/funnel/digital real estate + **domain-specific knowledge** that makes what you do more valuable than the next person. The generalist/polymath that AI accelerates ([[concepts/polymathy]]) becomes an entire operation of one.

Naval, quoted by Koe: "There are almost 7B people on this planet. Someday, I hope, there will be almost 7B companies."

Koe's sharpening of "creator": **not** influencer / "personal brand" as an aesthetic — someone who **creates value** (solves problems, ships solutions, helps others do the same) and gets paid because value exchange is the crux of human relationships. James Clear as the model: the brand is just the leverage vessel to get eyes on the life's work. "A personal brand is the highest-leverage vessel to be a creator right now."

## Why it matters here (= his vision, named)
This is the **structural form of the human's [[vision]]** — "a world where everyone has their own thing / their own business / their own agency." The one-human business is what "everyone has their own thing" *looks like* operationally. His [[fms]] product teaches the on-ramp to it (learn to create online from scratch → your own one-human business).
