# Concept: Job → Career → Calling

Dan Koe's progression of work, by how much agency and self it demands. Source: [[sources/koe-purpose-and-profit]] (ch. 2).

## The three (as Koe defines them)
- **Job** — "unpleasant work you do for someone else for the sole purpose of making money." A survival mechanism. Fine for the young or those who need to survive.
- **Career** — "a commitment to development in your work." A hierarchy of challenging roles that forces self-development. "Extended schooling."
- **Calling** — "work you can't pull yourself away from and others can't help but pay you for." Can't be assigned; "found at the point where improvement turns into obsession." Your mind is always on it.

Nesting: a calling is a career is a job (upward), but not downward — a job isn't a career; a career isn't a calling.

## The human's working version
The calling is **aligned work** ([[concepts/alignment]]) — where earning, curiosity, and self point the same way. It's what [[fms]] helps people reach, and the end-state of [[concepts/levels-of-purpose]]. Koe's arc-line that anchors it: "At the start, you create to make money. In the end, you make money to create."
