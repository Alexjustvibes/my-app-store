# Concept: The Decentralized Education System (social media)

Social media reframed as a **decentralized education system** — knowledge transfer that's peer-to-peer, permissionless, and outside institutions. Posting isn't vanity; it's *teaching* in the new university.

## The human's working version
From [[sources/own-ideas-2026-06-09]]:
- "One of the biggest things you can do for the prosperity of society is **adding your specific knowledge to the decentralized education system** (social media)." → publishing is a civic/contributive act, not just self-promotion.
- "Every email you add to your list is **an additional student who seeks your instruction.**" → the email list is a *classroom*; subscribers are students, not metrics.

So: you're a professor in the decentralized university. Your content = lectures, your list = classroom, your product ([[fms]]) = the course.

## How it sits in the wiki
- It's [[concepts/institutions-to-individuals]] applied specifically to *education* — credibility/teaching moving from universities to individuals with proof of work.
- It's the positive frame of [[seeds/apprenticeship-not-school]] and [[seeds/credentials-are-depreciating-assets]]: if the institution isn't where learning happens anymore, *this* is.
- It gives [[concepts/media-as-leverage]] a mission: the "own your email list" rule becomes "tend your classroom."
- **Mission-core:** "adding your specific knowledge for the prosperity of society" is *helping people free themselves* via knowledge — the north star, stated as a teaching act. [[fms]] is the human's own node in this system.
