# Concept: Levels of Purpose

Dan Koe's four developmental stages of *why* you work / make money. Source: [[sources/koe-purpose-and-profit]] (ch. 6). You don't escape lower levels — you **integrate** them. Each is more selfless, less selfish than the last.

## The four
1. **Survival** — doing the assigned thing to get by; mind stuck in bills/stress/the job you don't want. Money looks hard, lucky, or evil.
2. **Status** — security around money; a skill/business that pays; risk of getting trapped in the materialist/prestige paradigm. (Koe: this stage is necessary — "I am giving you permission to pursue your desire for acceptance through power." You find spirituality *in* the journey, not by skipping to it.)
3. **Creativity** — the superficial gets old; you build your *own* way from accumulated experience; "your job in the creativity stage is to simply create."
4. **Contribution** — work/rest/play collapse into one; "your entire life begins to revolve around how you can best contribute"; you become a "perspective vessel for reality."

## The human's working version
This is the developmental backbone under [[concepts/alignment]] and [[concepts/developmental-stages]] — and the *why*-side of [[concepts/job-career-calling]]. The mission ("aligned freedom / free others") is the **contribution** level. Practical use: the [[sources/koe-purpose-and-profit#study-questions|study question]] — *which level am I actually operating from in work? in money?* Honest answer guides the next move.

## Caveat (Koe's own)
Don't skip steps — "you will experience significant emotional backlash if you try to skip steps." Trying to jump to creativity/contribution (or "spirituality") without the survival/status reps is itself a status game.
