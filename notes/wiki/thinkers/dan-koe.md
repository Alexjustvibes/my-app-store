# Thinker: Dan Koe

The creator-economy writer/educator behind the "one-person business" and "2 Hour Writer / 2 Hour Content" material. A core recurring mind for this wiki — his process models map closely onto the human's own frameworks, which makes him both a source of scaffolding and a sparring partner.

## Core claims (as they appear so far)
- Writing is a **balance of creativity and productivity**, run as two distinct modes ([[concepts/two-modes-of-attention]]). See [[sources/dan-koe-creativity-vs-productivity]].
- Most "writing" is upstream of typing — it happens in idea generation and outlining.
- **Brainstorm, then let it simmer**: rest activates the subconscious, which does the connective work. Outline a week ahead; write spaced out.
- "Good writing cannot be forced." Creativity = connection; productivity = clarity.
- Business has a spiritual / "do nothing" dimension (otium by another name — [[concepts/otium-negotium]]).
- References a **2 Hour Content ecosystem** as the practical system this theory feeds (referenced, not yet detailed in ingested material).
- **Domain of Mastery** (what to write about): three interests — money-making, genuine, developmental — whose overlap makes writing sustainable. See [[frameworks/domain-of-mastery]] / [[sources/koe-what-do-you-write-about]]. "Writing is how you connect the dots" echoes his stars→constellation metaphor in [[sources/dan-koe-creativity-vs-productivity]].
- **Who you write for = you** (past/present/future self; "3rd-person public journal"); your avatar is you, take the MBTI, list the problems that keep you up at night. See [[sources/koe-who-are-you-writing-for]].
- **Social Leverage** — build a brand over 6-12 months on 3 pillars: growth, authority (depth + social proof), authenticity. See [[frameworks/social-leverage]].
- **Purpose & Profit** (his book — the human's "bible" and the **master source** behind everything above; the separately-ingested modules are downstream of it): job→career→calling, agency, levels of purpose, problems-are-the-path (Deutsch), Nature's Compass, the 10 pillars, writing as the meta-skill, "you are the niche," consumer→creator. "Free yourself, then free others." See [[sources/koe-purpose-and-profit]].
- **The Experience Model** (his, though listed under the human's "Mine"): your lived experience is the "why"; self-development is the supply side; level up to new [[concepts/developmental-stages|stages]] and teach them; "your niche is you." See [[sources/koe-the-experience-model]] / [[frameworks/the-experience-model]]. _(Note: much of the human's writing system is Koe-lineage — APAG/PPP/Levels of Awareness/Experience Model.)_
- "Entrepreneurship is self-improvement in disguise." (from [[sources/commonplace-2026-06-09]]) — the one-person business as a personal-development vehicle, not just an income one. _(The human later reposted this line as his own note — [[sources/own-notes-batch-pre-wiki]] — so it's a belief he's adopted, not just recorded.)_
- **The Digital Renaissance Man / polymathy** — the AI age rewards the multi-dimensional generalist who channels many interests into **one craft**, not the specialist or the AI-purist. Craft = *care × unique ability × what others pay for*, built as a **3-layer skill stack** (human-nature foundation → domain-obsession combination → automatable tool skill). "The mechanical part gets automated; the human role moves up a layer." Second Renaissance: "proof of work replaces the resume, an audience replaces the employer, taste replaces credentials." See [[sources/koe-digital-renaissance-polymaths]] / [[concepts/polymathy]] / [[concepts/one-human-business]].
- **On AI itself** (notable — Koe is neither refusenik nor maximalist): AI has *no perspective* ("a bag of water you must mold"); use it for the mechanical layer (research, code, first drafts), keep taste/judgment human. He deliberately **keeps writing manual** ("the value of writing is in the reader's eyes; I want fine-tuned control") while automating research — a clean model for the human's own AI-build lane ([[dealership-content-lane]]).

## Where I diverge
> _(Human to fill / confirm — these are candidate divergences surfaced at ingest, not asserted positions.)_
- **The mechanism is borrowed and he admits it.** Koe says outright he "hasn't researched this much." The DMN/TPN and 11M-bits framing is pop-neuroscience layered onto a practice he found empirically. The practice survives without the story — which raises the question of why creators reach for borrowed scientific authority at all. See [[seeds/working-practice-wrong-explanation]].
- **"Spirituality in business" is doing a lot of undefined work.** Where Koe gestures at spirituality, the human's vocabulary (otium/negotium, reenchantment, fallibilism) is more precise. Naming it that way may be the more original move than Koe's softer framing.
- Possible divergence on the "phone ready to capture the idea" reflex — capture-everything can fight the very rest state that produced the idea. (Open question, not a settled disagreement.)
