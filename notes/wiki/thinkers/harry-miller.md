# Thinker: Harry Miller

An essayist the human is reading — sharp, literary, culturally diagnostic. A voice for **generational disillusionment**: the broken meritocracy, the credential trap, institutional decay, the hunger for agency. Useful as a *diagnostician* of the disease the human's mission offers the cure for.

## Core claims (so far, across 2 essays)
- **The meritocratic promise is broken.** "I played by the rules, and I got played." Doing everything right (grades, credentials, obedience) no longer pays out. See [[sources/harry-miller-hypnosis-of-high-achievement]].
- **Powerlessness is the modern wound.** Power = seeing your actions affect your environment; the weak "exist without effect— inconsequentially." The disenfranchised then borrow power by belonging to groups and become "zealous rule followers."
- **Most achievement is busy work** — "lifetime achievements in a worthless, virtual game." (Echoes Kaczynski's surrogate activities, his recurring reference.)
- **Action over optimization/perfectionism** (essay 2, "Beauty out of Good"): "Anything you can do without failure is well below your means"; "perfectionism is cowardice." See [[sources/beauty-out-of-good-essay]].
- "Attention is a thing that you pay with."

## Where I diverge
> _(Candidate divergences — human to confirm.)_
- **He diagnoses; he doesn't build the exit.** Harry's essays end in disillusioned anger and a vague "spark to start over." The human's mission supplies what Harry's lament is missing: a *constructive* path out — make something, permissionless leverage, [[concepts/alignment|aligned freedom]], [[fms]]. **The human's content is the answer to Harry's complaint.**
- **His broader worldview is off the mission** — the "Beauty out of Good" essay tilts into physiognomy, gender essentialism, civilizational-collapse / aristocracy-vs-democracy politics, and approving Kaczynski citations. That worldview is NOT the human's (the mission is freedom *for everyone*, not hierarchy/decline). Mine Harry for the *diagnosis and the prose energy*; leave the ideology. (Flagged on [[sources/beauty-out-of-good-essay]].)
