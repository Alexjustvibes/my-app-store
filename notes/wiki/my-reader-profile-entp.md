The audience blueprint. Per [[frameworks/the-experience-model]], the most accurate audience is **your past, present, and future self.** The human is an **ENTP**, so his reader is an ENTP-like mind a step behind him — and his greatest problems *are* the reader's burning problems *are* the content. This page is a living blueprint.

This page is exactly that exercise — built before reading the instruction.

## Honesty caveat (keep it on-brand)
MBTI isn't rigorous science — it's a useful *lens*, not gospel. Treat it the way [[seeds/working-practice-wrong-explanation]] treats borrowed psychology: a heuristic that's practically useful even if the mechanism is shaky. Use it as a map; drop any part that doesn't fit the territory.

## The ENTP wiring (quick)
- **Ne (dominant)** — idea generation, possibilities, connecting distant fields. (His polymathy, his "connect ancient to future," the whole seed factory.)
- **Ti (auxiliary)** — logical frameworks, first principles. (His love of frameworks/systems.)
- **Fe (tertiary)** — reading people, persuasion/recognition. (His instinct for [[concepts/resonance]].)
- **Si (inferior, the weak spot)** — routine, consistency, follow-through, *finishing.* This is where the problems live.

## His greatest problems (the blueprint — and the seed fuel)
Each is the ENTP pattern + his *demonstrated* version this session. These double as the "paint the enemy" material in [[frameworks/apag]].
1. **Starts everything, ships little** (inferior Si). Evidence: the shipping-guard flag — many seeds, few shipped; backburnered projects (MindHybrid, No Man's World, etc. per CLAUDE.md). The core wound.
2. **Too many interests, can't commit to one** (dominant Ne). Evidence: 4+ interests, "honestly I'm not sure what my genuine interest is," wanting more than Koe's 3 ([[my-domain-of-mastery]]).
3. **Input outruns output** — idea-hoarding, reading > making. Evidence: this very reading spree; the intake-heavy session.
4. **Novelty-seeking** — abandons a project once the *interesting* part is solved and the boring execution begins (Ne bored by Si).
5. **The Debater** — contrarian takes are his gift (his seed template is literally "counterintuitive claim"), but the shadow is arguing for sport / contrarian-for-its-own-sake. The healthy form is the Jester-Hero truth-teller ([[frameworks/the-jester-hero]]); the unhealthy form is just being difficult.
6. **Resists routine/structure** (Si) — yet structure is exactly what saves him. This wiki, the pipeline, the Outline System are the external Si he doesn't have internally.

## Who I'm writing for
An ENTP (or Ne-dominant) one stage behind: **brilliant, idea-rich, and scattered** — overflowing with potential and starts, unable to finish, drowning in their own inputs, never having shipped the thing. Their pain is his pain. Speak to that person and he's speaking to himself.

## The convergence (why "writing for myself" works)
The three things line up on one point:
- **His psychology:** the ENTP can't *finish* (inferior Si).
- **His product:** [[fms]] = **"Finally Make Something"** — which literally names that wound.
- **His audience:** ENTPs who can't ship.

So *writing for himself = writing for ENTPs = the exact people FMS is built to save.* The product name is the ENTP's core problem stated as a promise. That's why this avatar isn't a marketing exercise — it's the same person three times over.
