## One-line
Pick three overlapping interests — monetizable, genuine, and developmental — then mine each (principles → topics → mentors → ideas → problems) so you never run out of aligned things to write about.

## Key claims
1. **Writing is for thinking, not just attention** — "writing is how you connect the dots"; treat it as a vessel for self-, skill-, and interest-mastery.
2. **The Domain of Mastery = 3 interests:** money-making + genuine + developmental. Their overlap makes writing "seamless." → [[frameworks/domain-of-mastery]].
3. **Eternal markets:** health, wealth, relationships, happiness → sub-markets → niche-markets. Broaden up a stage to grow audience; don't box yourself in too soon.
4. **Be decisive:** "Pick something… you can change it at any time. Second-guessing only delays you." Anti-paralysis.
5. **You're not that special:** "If you are interested in something, other people are." Kills the "too niche" excuse.
6. **The developmental interest:** study development (psychology/philosophy/spirituality) → understand yourself → understand others → write with impact.
7. **The "why" is your greatest tool** — it sets you apart and displays your unique perspective.
