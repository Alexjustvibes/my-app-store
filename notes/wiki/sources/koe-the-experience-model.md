## One-line
Your unique lived experience (not your interests) is what people care about; build on a foundation of self-development, level up to new *stages*, and teach the stage you've reached — "your niche is you."

## Key claims
1. Interests = the "how," lived experience = the "why." People care how it improves *their* life.
2. **Domain of Mastery is a vessel for self-development** (esp. the mind); self-development → unlimited content ideas; "personal growth = business growth."
3. **States vs. stages** (Wilber): level up to a permanent new stage → that's what others will pay to reach. → [[concepts/developmental-stages]].
4. Markets desaturate as people evolve → the creator economy can't be saturated.
5. **Become a Sense Maker; content as a map.** Document your findings for 3-5 years.
6. **Passion for life itself**, not 1-3 things; take nothing as law — run it through your own experience.
7. **"Your niche is you."** Go broad to build a leverageable audience fast.

## Why the human flagged it ("why reading/leveling-up aids my goals")
This is the **justification for the reading spree** — and it resolves the earlier "is the wiki/reading part of the build?" tension. Koe's answer: yes — leveling up your mind *is* the supply side, because (a) you teach the stage you reached, (b) understanding yourself → understanding others → higher-impact writing, (c) each new stage desaturates the market and opens the next rung. **Captured as [[concepts/developmental-stages]].** Caveat held: a *state* (dopamine binge) isn't a *stage* (a metabolized new baseline you then document) — the in/out ratio still applies.
