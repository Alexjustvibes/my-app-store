## One-line
The overcredentialed golden boy who did everything right — valedictorian, 4.0 engineering, Rhodes finalist — can't get a job better than minimum-wage at Crunch Fitness, and wakes up to the fact that the meritocratic promise was a hypnosis.

## Key claims
1. **"I played by the rules, and I got played."** Two decades of grades, obedience, and extracurriculars bought a broken inheritance, not a living wage.
2. **The institutional path is a dead loop** — the firehouse "did you apply online / try again in 5 years"; the "perfect match" job demanding 3 years' experience for entry-level; the AI résumé-screener that trashes you over a missing keyword.
3. **Powerlessness is the core wound** — power = your actions visibly affecting your world; the weak "exist without effect, inconsequentially." Absent that, people borrow power by joining groups and prize being "a zealous rule follower… a protocol droid."
4. **Most achievement is busy work** ("lab reports for experiments first written in 1997") — surrogate goals in "a worthless, virtual game."
5. **Waking up** — the generation sees the moving goalposts and wants "autonomy, sovereignty, dignity, and efficacy."
