What *was* extracted is a narrow strand that genuinely serves the mission: the critique of **optimization/self-help as avoidance**, and the closing **call to action over perfectionism**.

## What furthers the cause (the on-mission strand)
1. **Optimization is avoidance dressed as virtue.** "Bed → Gym → Work → Supermarket. Repeat. No room... to create, much less be of service." → external articulation of [[seeds/self-improvement-made-you-unrelatable]] (self-improvement that serves no one and isolates).
2. **"The Beautiful Ones" (Calhoun) + the Last Man.** Self-absorbed males who groom and withdraw while everything collapses; the man who "invented happiness" and blinks. → a vivid image of the *consumer who optimizes the self but creates nothing* — exactly the withdrawn ENTP in [[my-reader-profile-entp]], and the person [[fms]] ("Finally Make Something") exists to save.
3. **Perfectionism is cowardice.** "All those drafts collecting dust... you're not editing, you're dilly dallying." / "Anything you can do without failure is well below your means." → the sharpest external fuel yet for the shipping guard / [[concepts/the-arena]] / [[seeds/unused-creative-power-is-destructive]]. Action over deliberation.
4. **"Beauty out of Good" — results as a byproduct, not a target.** Pursue beauty directly and it collapses; live rightly and it appears. → maps onto [[concepts/resonance]] (can't be manufactured, only earned) and [[concepts/alignment]] (outcomes follow from right action, not from chasing the outcome).
5. **The mind needs the body to make thought real** — "a thought that can't be made real may as well not exist." → execution over ideation; the [[concepts/the-arena]] / [[seeds/the-constraint-was-never-the-code]] point (ideas are worthless unmanifested).
6. **Self-help's contradiction** — "the whole implication of help is that it comes from beyond." → quiet support for the teaching frame ([[concepts/decentralized-education]]): real help is given to/from others, not hoarded in the self.
