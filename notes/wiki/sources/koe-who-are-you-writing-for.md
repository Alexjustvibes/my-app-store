## One-line
Write to your past/present/future self (your customer avatar *is* you — take the MBTI test, list the problems that keep you up at night), and build a brand on three pillars over 6-12 months: growth, authority, authenticity.

## Key claims
1. **Write to your past/present/future self** — a "3rd-person public journal." Advice to past self (traps), present self (what to do), future self (motivation). → extends [[frameworks/the-experience-model]].
2. **Your customer avatar is you.** The most important part of any avatar is *the list of problems that keep them up at night* — and for a personal brand, those problems are *yours.* He recommends the **MBTI test** to surface them.
3. **Don't box into one niche/avatar** — a single specific avatar (service to a person) limits the flexibility to write whatever you want and enjoy the work for life.
4. **Social Leverage:** perception is built over **6-12 months**, not one post. Three pillars — growth, authority (depth + social proof), authenticity (document projects, opinions, vulnerability). → [[frameworks/social-leverage]].
5. **Single-pillar accounts get ignored** and often hate the process (can't talk about what energizes them).
