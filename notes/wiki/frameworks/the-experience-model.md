# Framework (studied — Dan Koe; adopted by the human): The Experience Model

Type: **Dan Koe's framework**, adopted into the human's writing system. (CLAUDE.md lists it under "Mine" — provenance corrected 2026-06-09: it originates with Koe, see [[sources/koe-the-experience-model]]. Likely true of several of the human's "Mine" frameworks — APAG/PPP/Levels of Awareness are Koe-lineage too. The human's originality is in *application/synthesis*, not the frameworks themselves — worth holding for the "where I diverge" question.)
Definition source: the writing-coach skill + [[sources/koe-the-experience-model]] (canonical).
Use: decide **whose** life the writing serves and **which** audience it attracts.

## The model (as I use it)
- People care about **how your interests will improve their life** — not the interests themselves.
- Your **lifestyle and lived experience attract a specific, aligned audience.**
- **Write to your past self** (one stage behind where you are now). That is your most accurate audience.

> **Extended (from [[sources/koe-who-are-you-writing-for]]):** write to your past *and present and future* self — a "3rd-person public journal." Past self = traps to avoid; present self = what to do now; future self = motivation from the one who's already done it. And: **your customer avatar IS you** — the most important part of any avatar is the list of *problems that keep them up at night*, which for a personal brand are *your* problems. (This is exactly what [[my-reader-profile-entp]] does.)

## Why it's load-bearing
This is the operational form of the core belief that *the writer's unique life experience is the differentiator — not the topic* ([[frameworks/content-philosophy]]). The topic is the "how"; the lived experience is the "why."

## The fuller model (from the canonical source)
From [[sources/koe-the-experience-model]]:
- **Interests are the "how"; your lived experience is the "why."** People care how your interests improve *their* life. Hook with experience, then educate.
- **Domain of Mastery = a vessel for self-development (esp. mental).** "Everything connects… all worthwhile paths lead to life itself." Self-development → unlimited content ideas; "personal growth = business growth."
- **Developmental stages** (states vs. stages) → [[concepts/developmental-stages]]: level up to a new stage, then teach the stage to those behind you. The engine that makes leveling-up = the product.
- **Become a Sense Maker** — make sense of life for the people who align with you. **Content as a map** — "document everything you can make sense of" for 3-5 years.
- **Passion for life itself, not 1-3 things.** Don't take anything as law; run it through your own experience.
- **"Your niche is you."** Go broad (Experience Model + Domain of Mastery) to build a *leverageable* audience fast, vs. niching slow.

## How it wires into the rest of the system
- Supplies the **personal experience** required in [[frameworks/apag]] (Perspective/Advantage) and [[frameworks/ppp]] (Perspective).
- "Write to your past self" sets the [[frameworks/levels-of-awareness]] target — your past self is at a knowable awareness stage.
- "Aligned audience" connects to [[concepts/alignment]] and [[concepts/resonance]]: the frequency only your life could generate reaches the receiver it was meant for.
- It's the standing reason behind the hard rule that the human's published voice is never ghostwritten — the lived experience can't be outsourced.
