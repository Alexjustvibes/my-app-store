# Framework (studied — Dan Koe): Social Leverage

Type: others' framework the human is studying
Source: [[sources/koe-who-are-you-writing-for]] · [[thinkers/dan-koe]]
Use: build a brand worth more than its follower count, over a **6-12 month** time scale.

## The premise
One tweet/newsletter doesn't show your depth; neither does a month of them. **Your content over 6-12 months determines how people perceive you.** High social leverage = covering all 3 pillars → DM anyone and be taken seriously, close clients, get podcasts, transfer leverage across platforms. Single-pillar accounts (quotes/meme/super-niche) get ignored and struggle to earn — and often hate the process (can't talk about what energizes them).

## The 3 pillars
1. **Growth** — is the brand growing (followers/subs)? Driven by top-of-funnel content that gets *shared*, makes people click ("who is this guy?"), and is *polarizing through confidence* ([[frameworks/attention-capture-tactics]] — confidence/conviction). If not growing, study what's working and play to the algorithm a bit.
2. **Authority** = **depth + social proof.** Depth = deep understanding of one area → unique perspectives & compelling arguments; comes from **long-form** (newsletters/blogs). Social proof = credentials, accomplishments, testimonials. Beginners: establish authority fast with long-form + actionable advice. ("On Twitter but not writing newsletters = shooting yourself in the foot.")
3. **Authenticity** = documenting your projects (everything's a project — mental/physical/spiritual/financial development → lessons for those 2-3 steps behind) + expressing opinions/likes/dislikes + **vulnerability** (about losses and what you'll do differently — not pitiful).

Beginners: start with **growth + authority**; authenticity compounds in over time. Everything stays up forever, so you can pivot later.
