# Framework (studied — Dan Koe): Domain of Mastery

Type: others' framework the human is studying (not his own)
Source: [[sources/koe-what-do-you-write-about]] · [[thinkers/dan-koe]]
Use: **choose what to write about** — pick three interests whose overlap makes writing sustainable.

## The three interests
A writer should find the balance of three:
1. **Money-Making interest** — something monetizable you can do for a long time (so you can write full-time).
2. **Genuine interest** — what you're actually excited to learn about (browser/YouTube history, the books you buy). For Koe: spirituality & consciousness.
3. **Developmental interest** — the study of *development itself* (psychology, philosophy, spirituality, self-improvement) so you understand yourself → understand others → write with more impact.

When all three are met, "writing becomes seamless and life becomes fun."

## The Eternal Markets (how to find the money-making interest)
A descending hierarchy:
- **Eternal markets:** health, wealth, relationships, happiness. (Where the burning problems are.)
- **Sub-markets:** fitness, online business, dating, spirituality. (Best to build an audience around.)
- **Niche-markets:** bodybuilding, content creation, "game," mindfulness meditation. (Best to sell a product against a burning problem.)
- **Broaden, don't niche too soon:** move *up* a stage for audience size — talk about email marketing *to explain* marketing. Bodybuilding → health; meditation → spirituality.

## Mining an interest (the 5-part breakdown)
For any chosen interest:
1. **Principles / fundamentals** — the rules of the game; an evergreen foundation to point people to.
2. **Topics & sub-topics** — attack from all angles for depth/authority; each becomes short- and long-form ideas. (Break far enough and *everything connects* — people infer your expertise from the web of your content.)
3. **Mentors to emulate** — follow the proven, buy the top books = "interest immersion" → market research at your fingertips.
4. **Validated content ideas** — flood in from 1–3.
5. **Real-world problems** — yours and others', spotted once immersed → content angles.

Feeds the **2 Hour Content Ecosystem** (referenced again; still not detailed in any ingested source).
