# Framework: The One-Person Business Map (self → business)

Type: **adopted / studied — Dan Koe's**, not the human's own. A reference diagram he saved 2026-08-30 (see [[reference-images]]). The actionable, 8-box companion to the [[concepts/one-human-business]] concept.

## The map
Two columns. The **left is your own life**; the **right is the business** — and the right is a *projection* of the left. You don't invent a business; you translate your lived experience into one, row by row.

| YOUR LIFE (input) | → | THE BUSINESS (output) |
|---|---|---|
| **GOALS** — What do you want out of life? | → | **BRAND** — What are you leading people towards? |
| **PROBLEMS** — What is standing in your way? | → | **CONTENT** — What roadblocks will they encounter? |
| **SYSTEMS** — How did you solve the problem? | → | **PRODUCT** — How can they solve their problems faster? |
| **BENEFITS** — How has it impacted your life? | → | **MARKETING** — Why should they care about your message? |

> "Sensible people get paid for doing what they enjoy doing." — Alan Watts

## Why this matters (it's his whole wiki as a build-spec)
This is **"you are the niche" turned into an assembly diagram** ([[frameworks/the-experience-model]], [[seeds/solve-your-own-problems-first]]). The thesis: *your business is your own life-work, externalized.* Each left box is a page he already maintains — the right box is what it becomes when pointed at a reader:

- **GOALS → BRAND.** His [[goals]] / [[vision]] become the direction he leads people toward = the mission ([[concepts/alignment|aligned freedom]]). The brand isn't a logo; it's the destination.
- **PROBLEMS → CONTENT.** His [[problems-log]] *is* the content queue — literally. "What is standing in your way" (his problems) = "what roadblocks will they encounter" (the reader's, one stage behind). **Every entry in his problems-log is a piece of content waiting.** This is the single tightest mapping in his wiki.
- **SYSTEMS → PRODUCT.** How he solved each problem = the product that helps the reader solve it faster. [[fms]] is the systems-for-problem-#4/#8 (vision + shipping) packaged.
- **BENEFITS → MARKETING.** The transformation in his own life = the reason the reader should care. The proof *is* the pitch.

## How he uses it
It's a **content/product generator**, not a poster. Working order: solve a real problem in your life → write down the system → ship the benefit as content → the recurring problem becomes the product. Run any row of his [[problems-log]] through it and a content angle + a product falls out. Pairs with the [[frameworks/domain-of-mastery|Content Tree]] (that one generates *topics*; this one generates the *offer + the reason to buy*).
