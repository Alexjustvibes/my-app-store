# The Experience Model — Dan Koe

> Source: Dan Koe (course module). Continues the Domain of Mastery / Social Leverage series. Clipped by the human 2026-06-09. Immutable. (Faithful, lightly condensed.)

Theory, not step-by-step. "A seed of awareness to make sense of over time."

## The core
- Your **unique experience** pursuing your interests is what people care about — not the interests themselves. Interests are the **"how"**; your lifestyle/experience is the **"why."** People care how your interests make *their* life better.
- You hook people with aspects of your life experience, then educate them on how to get there (authority content, newsletter, lead magnet, product). You attract a unique following by talking about your lifestyle — the pains, benefits, emotions. This bridges "work" and "life."

## Domain of Mastery = a vessel for self-development (esp. MENTAL)
- A structured way of digging deeper into life and connecting its sub-components. "All worthwhile paths lead to the same endpoint: life itself. As you dig deeper… everything connects."
- Requires a foundation of self-development → unlimited unique content ideas. The mind is the most important pillar. "Expanding your mind = increasing your creative potential." You must become a "thought leader" — take yourself, your work, your life seriously.
- **Strong recommendation: make self-development / mental mastery your 3rd (developmental) interest.** The iceberg: psychology, spirituality, survival, metaphysics, human potential, philosophy, leadership, strategy, learning, creativity, cosmology, human behavior, performance & productivity.
- **"Once you understand how to develop yourself, you understand how to develop others. Personal growth = business growth."**
- Resources he recommends: Actualized.org; books *Flow* and *The Art of Impossible*.

## Developmental stages & removing saturation (Ken Wilber)
- **States** = temporary (felt creative for an hour). **Stages** = permanent (a new baseline level of creativity).
- Your job: dive into each interest and develop until you hit a **new stage.** "This new stage implies that other people want to reach it and will pay to do so." That's monetization — and how you write to someone one stage behind you (or your past self one stage behind).
- As you evolve, your content and products evolve. (Koe: "I no longer do freelance/coaching… reached a new stage… my products iterated into something completely different.")
- Each evolution **"desaturates" the market** — you open room for the next generation. This is why the one-person business / creator economy **can never be saturated**: individuals evolve and teach the next generation they align with.

## Become a Sense Maker
- "Your job is to make sense of life to the people that understand you." Provide clarity to those who align with your values/vision/goals/beliefs (that alignment forms your perspective).

## Find the lesson in every experience
- "Stop trying to find your passion. Instead, cultivate a passion for life itself" — all its highs and lows. It *should* be hard to be passionate about only 1-3 things; aim to be passionate about everything. Seeing depth in everything makes life 10x more enjoyable.
- **Don't take everything you learn as law** (tweets, books, gurus, parents, society). Run it through your own perspective, values, biases, and *experience*. "You were put on this earth to piece together the puzzle of reality — your writing is how you document your findings."
- **Content as a map.** For the next 3-5 years, document everything you can make sense of. "Your content is a map for navigating life from your unique perspective… a world you invite people into… the projection of your mind."

## Attracting like-minded people without a "niche"
- Target your former/current/future self → the perfect niche. Talk about your life with confidence and conviction → attract an ultra-specific person.
- Self-development is (Koe firmly believes) a must — part of his life philosophy; basic need in Maslow's. Some won't agree — "that is exactly my point" (they self-select out).
- Two options: **(1) niche slow** (hyper-targeted authority content; or just do direct outreach/resumes faster); **(2) leverageable broad fast** (Experience Model + Domain of Mastery) → attract 50-100K who know what you do and spread your name 5-10x. Build: educational content for beginners (low awareness), authority via your results/skills, growth via broad high-impact life content. "Think about the opportunities from 30K tweets, not the results from the 3 you post today."
- Example: copywriters who give beginner tips + lessons learned (broad) beat profiles that scream "I'll close you as a client."

## Summary / Insight: Your Niche Is You
- Don't shy from posting beliefs, ideas, experiments, insights. The Dickie tweet example hits: pattern interrupt (squiggly lines), numbers, implied transformation, confidence/conviction (polarizes), lifestyle flex (a journal in the image).
- Don't be neurotic about only posting your expertise — that's not how you build a timeless brand (but expertise still feeds the 3 pillars of social leverage). Twitter = the testing ground; cross-post the best.
- **"Your niche is you."** You are the leader of your followers — people like you who want to do what you do.
