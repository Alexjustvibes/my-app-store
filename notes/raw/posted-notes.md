## 2026-08-30 · "Ideas are the new oil" · TikTok (video, spoken) · 65 likes (best to date)
Ideas are the new oil. Now that AI is out and people are starting to create things just from saying it, just from expressing it into the world — and having the artificial intelligence create things for them — it's come to the attention of many people that raw skill, raw understanding of how to create things, isn't what holds you back. It's the idea underneath it. It's the fact that you can't just tell an AI, "hey Claude, make me the next awesome idea and make me a billion dollars." You can't just say that. You actually have to create a new, original idea for your brain, because the AI can't do that for you anymore. So where is the value now? It's your ideas. That's why writing, and writing things down, and forming new ideas — creating new idea experiments within your mind, within the writing itself — is what is going to be the most valuable going forward. Because you could just write a whole idea file of what you want the AI to create, and it will just make it. You don't have to do shit. So yeah — ideas are now the most valuable part of the process when you create basically anything that's valuable now.
