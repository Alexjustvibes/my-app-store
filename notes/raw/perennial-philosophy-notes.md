# Perennial Philosophy — Core Notes (Alex's study notes)

> Source: the human's own study/synthesis notes. Dropped 2026-06-09. His genuine-interest domain (philosophy/psychology). Immutable.

## Definition
Perennial philosophy: beneath surface differences of the world's religious/mystical traditions (Vedanta, Sufism, Christian mysticism, Kabbalah, Taoism, Buddhism, Neoplatonism), there's a shared core structure about reality, consciousness, and the divine. Popularized by Aldous Huxley (1945); earlier narrow use by Leibniz. Traditionalist School (Guénon, Schuon, Coomaraswamy) = stricter "one Primordial Tradition" version. Ken Wilber = developmental/stage version (Integral Theory).

## Core claims
1. A transcendent ground underlies the visible world.
2. That ground can be known directly (not just believed) via contemplative practice.
3. The true self, at depth, is continuous with / identical to that ground.
4. Life's purpose is realizing this — fragmented ego-awareness → union/awakening.
5. Ethics/compassion flow naturally from realization, not from external rules.

## The Great Chain of Being
Matter → Life → Mind (ego/reason) → Soul (archetypal/intuitive) → Spirit (undivided ground). Most people live at Mind, mistaking ego for the whole self. Renamed across traditions: Vedanta's koshas, Kabbalah's four worlds, Neoplatonism's matter→soul→Nous→the One, Sufism's nafs→qalb→ruh→sirr.

## Self/Ground identity claim
- Vedanta: Atman is Brahman ("tat tvam asi"); separation = maya from avidya.
- Buddhism: anatta (no fixed self) — opposite route, similar destination (liberation from separateness).
- Christian mysticism (Eckhart): the soul's "spark"/ground is uncreated, same in kind as God.
- Sufism: fana (ego annihilation), sometimes → baqa (return transformed).
- Taoism: alignment with the Tao; self stops resisting the flow.

## Three-stage path
Purgation → Illumination → Union. Recurs across Christian mystical theology, Sufi maqamat, Vedantic/Buddhist schemas (Buddhism reframes "union" as cessation).

## Method convergences
- Stilling the mind (meditation, hesychasm/Jesus Prayer, dhikr, zazen).
- Apophatic/negative method (neti neti, Tao Te Ching opening, Pseudo-Dionysius, Cloud of Unknowing).
- Ethical purification as prerequisite to insight.

## Jung's psychological translation
- "God" of perennialism relocated inside the psyche: collective unconscious = shared transcendent ground.
- Archetypes (Shadow, Anima/Animus, Self, Wise Old Man) = psychologized Great Chain.
- The Self (capital S) = archetype of wholeness = Atman/Buddha-nature/Eckhart's spark; mandala = its signature.
- Individuation = psychologized purgation/illumination/union (Shadow → Anima/Animus → Self). Jung preferred ego-Self relationship over full ego-dissolution.
- Sources: alchemy (lapis = realized Self), Gnosticism, Secret of the Golden Flower, I Ching.
- Synchronicity / unus mundus: psyche + matter as two aspects of one reality.

## Instinct ↔ archetype
Archetype = "the instinct's image of itself." Instinct = biological pole; archetype = symbolic pole (attachment instinct ↔ Mother archetype). Psychoid level = psyche + biology not yet separated. Naturalistic mechanism for cross-cultural convergence (shared biology → shared symbol). Evo-psych accepts universal predispositions, skeptical of inherited specific symbolic content.

## Master list of universal ideas
1. Reality is layered (Great Chain). 2. Unconditioned ground beneath conditioned things. 3. Ground unmoved yet causes all movement (wu wei, Unmoved Mover, actus purus). 4. Ultimate reality escapes language (apophatic). 5. Ordinary awareness = sleep/ignorance (maya, avidya, Plato's cave). 6. True self deeper than ego, continuous with ultimate reality. 7. Direct experiential knowing > belief/argument (gnosis, jnana, ma'rifa, prajna). 8. Purgation → illumination → union. 9. Ethics/compassion arise from realization. 10. Light as symbol of spiritual knowing. 11. Personal/impersonal paradox (Absolute as both "Thou" and ground). 12. Cyclical time / cosmic rhythm. 13. Death-and-rebirth as master metaphor for transformation. 14. Sacrifice of the small (ego-will) for the great. 15. World as veil/dream/play/emanation. 16. Microcosm/macrocosm ("as above, so below"). 17. Number/geometry as cosmic order. 18. Grace/unearned illumination alongside effort.

**Meta-pattern:** not 18 facts — one recurring shape (**fragmentation → dissolution → reintegration**) at every scale: cosmology, psychology, ethics, practice, narrative.

## Hero's Journey (Campbell) = the narrative version of the shape
Monomyth (1949, Jung-influenced) = individuation as story. Departure (Call → Refusal → Aid → Threshold → Belly of the Whale/symbolic death) · Initiation (Road of Trials → Atonement → Apotheosis → Ultimate Boon) · Return (Refusal → Magic Flight → Return Threshold → Master of Two Worlds → Freedom to Live). Mapping: Belly of Whale = symbolic death; Road of Trials = purgation; Apotheosis = illumination; Boon = union; Return = compassion/service (bodhisattva, Sufi baqa). **The Return is myth's key addition — pure union has no built-in obligation to come back and share it; myth insists on it.**

## Taoism as stress test
Fits: unmoved ground (wu wei), purest apophatic opening, wu wei = ego-surrender (≈ fana/karma yoga), internal alchemy (jing→qi→shen→emptiness = purgation/illumination/union in physiology), most literal micro/macro tradition. Breaks: no hero's-journey quest (sage doesn't quest/return with a boon; ideal = "uncarved block," return to simplicity via subtraction — "in pursuit of Tao, every day something is dropped"); minimal personal/impersonal paradox; cyclical time without apocalyptic climax. Takeaway: confirms deep structures but rejects acquisition-through-ordeal narrative — counterweight against over-applying the hero's-journey lens.

## Standing critiques
- Cherry-picking / quote-mining passages that rhyme; understating doctrinal conflict.
- Steven Katz: mystical experience is never "pure" — always pre-shaped by the mystic's concepts (a Christian and Buddhist mystic may have genuinely different experiences).
- Flattening: "you're all saying the same thing" erases non-negotiable commitments.
- Same critique applies to Campbell's monomyth (stretchable onto any story with enough relabeling).

## Practical uses of the lens
1. Diagnostic scaffold for unfamiliar mystical/philosophical texts. 2. Antidote to religious tribalism/literalism. 3. Interpretive tool for dreams, art, recurring symbols in fiction. 4. Map for personal contemplative practice without full doctrinal commitment (most-criticized use — risk of shallow syncretism). 5. Bridge to modern research on meditation/psychedelics/"ego dissolution." 6. Corrective both ways (nudges scientism toward taking convergence seriously; nudges literalism toward humility about local dressing).

High-leverage because independently rediscovered across disconnected cultures (a filter for something structurally real, not a local quirk).
